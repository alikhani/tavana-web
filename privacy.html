Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02 · Version: 1.0

1. The short version

Tavana is a training app that adapts your day's training to how you feel. To do that we process information about you, and some of it concerns your health. We want you to know what it is, why we have it and what you can do about it.

  • We process health data only with your explicit consent. You give it in the app and can withdraw it there (section 4).
  • We do not sell your data and we show no advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your data is stored in the EU. Some providers are US companies (section 8).
  • The coach chat and AI-written insights are switched off in this version. No health data is sent to any AI service. A few features use an AI service without health data (section 6).
  • You can export your data and delete your account in the app (sections 9 and 10).
  • Tavana gives training guidance, not medical advice.
  • You must be at least 18.

2. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is:

  • Nima Alikhani, who develops and runs Tavana as a private individual.
  • Contact for privacy questions: support@tavana.app
  • No data protection officer has been appointed.

3.6 Product statistics and improvement

We store a few simple events about how the app is used, to know whether it works. Examples: that a first workout was completed, which onboarding step was shown, whether today's suggestion was shown, opened, accepted, started or completed, whether you tapped "helpful" or "too hard" on a suggestion, which app version you run, and which day you opened the app (without time of day).

  • The events are stored with your account ID and are therefore not anonymous. They contain no health values and no free text.
  • We use no third-party analytics tools for this.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in developing and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • You can object to this processing (section 10).

3.7 Errors and crashes

We use Sentry to learn when the app crashes or our server fails. Our Sentry account is in Sentry's EU region (Germany).

  • In the app, no user details are sent (no name, no email and no account ID), and no screenshots or view hierarchies are attached. The app sends technical information about errors and crashes (for example device model, operating system, app version and internal ID numbers such as a workout ID), plus performance measurements for 10 percent of sessions, profiling for 5 percent of sessions, and technical logs.
  • On the server, everything sent to Sentry is filtered through a list of allowed fields. Request content, cookies, query parameters, email addresses and IP addresses are not sent. Error messages that may quote user text are redacted, and other error messages are shortened. Your account ID may be included so that we can troubleshoot.
  • Automatic breadcrumbs may contain the addresses of the app's requests to our server, with internal ID numbers, but not the content of the requests. Sentry is configured not to store IP addresses.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.8 Notifications

The app only shows local notifications on your phone, for example when a goal or a scheduled workout has been recorded. We send no push notifications and store no push token. You control notifications in iOS settings.

3.9 Technical logs

Our server logs, for each request: method, path, status code, response time and a request ID. Your IP address is used to limit the number of requests and protect against abuse, but is not written to our logs. Our hosting provider Fly.io processes IP addresses as part of running its service.

4. Health data and consent

Information about your health is a special category of personal data. We process it only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). This includes sleep, heart rate, HRV, breathing, illness level, cycle data, injury information, heart rate values from workouts, and what you write about your health in notes.

How you give consent. After you sign in, the app shows a consent screen before you can continue. It explains which health data is used, and you make two choices:

  • that you agree that Tavana processes your health data to adapt your training, and
  • that you are 18 or older.

Both are required to use the app, because adapting to your daily readiness is what the app does. If you choose "I don't agree", you can change your mind later, sign out, or delete your account right away. We store your choice with time and text version (section 3.1).

Apple Health is a separate choice. iOS asks permission for each type of data in Apple Health. That is not the same as the consent above, and you can change it at any time in iOS under Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices. Cycle tracking is off until you turn it on in the app.

How to withdraw consent. In the app: Settings, Privacy, "Withdraw health data consent". We then delete immediately:

  • your daily readiness, check-ins and timestamped readiness events,
  • heart rate on your workouts (the workouts stay),
  • your injury or limitation setting and the text you wrote about it,
  • today's suggestion and your weekly insights,
  • copies of this data on the phone.

Your workouts, sets and weights stay. The app cannot be used again until you give consent again. Withdrawal does not affect what was done before.

5. Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you allow it, the app reads from Apple Health: steps, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, respiratory rate, active calories, workouts with heart rate and distance, date of birth and biological sex. Menstrual data is read only if you turn on cycle tracking. The app does not read VO2 max, flights climbed, body weight or height. The app can write workouts, body weight and height to Apple Health.

All of this remains in Apple Health on your phone. Only what the service needs is sent to our servers:

  • Sent: sleep (hours and stages), resting heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate, steps and active calories, date of birth and sex, and your workouts (type, times, duration, energy, heart rate, distance, source).
  • Not sent to our servers: your weight and height, raw menstrual data and heart rate curves.

We do not use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, and we do not pass it to data brokers. This also applies to the Apple Watch app, which reads heart rate and energy from workouts recorded on the watch.

6. How recommendations are made, and where AI is used

Rule-based, in our own code. Today's status (green, amber or red), what is suggested on the Today screen, progression (when weight or repetitions increase), the weekly insights and the injury safety stop are calculated with fixed rules that we have written. No AI decides this. If you flag an injury or limitation, automatic session suggestions are paused until you turn it off.

No AI coach in this version. The coach chat and AI-written insights are switched off. No health data is sent to any AI service.

AI without health data. A few features use the Claude language model from Anthropic (USA) to write training content. Only what is listed below is sent to Anthropic. Your name, email address and account ID are never included.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy (Art. 15),
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16). You can change most of it yourself in the app,
  • have your data erased (Art. 17). Do it in the app or contact us,
  • restrict processing (Art. 18),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21),
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format and move it (data portability, Art. 20),
  • withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)), see section 4,
  • not be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22). See section 6.

Export your data. In the app: Settings, Privacy, Your data, "Export my data". You get a JSON file with what we hold about you: profile, consent choices, workouts, readiness, plans and other data linked to your account. The file contains health data, so keep it somewhere safe.

Other requests. Write to support@tavana.app from the email address you use in the app, and say what you want. We reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest. We may need to ask you to confirm it is you. If you cannot use the app, we can produce the export for you.

Complaints. If you think we process your data incorrectly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), Box 8114, 104 20 Stockholm, Sweden, www.imy.se, imy@imy.se. You can also contact the supervisory authority in the country where you live.

11. Security

This is what we do, and it is in our code and operational configuration:

  • All traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS (the server enforces HTTPS).
  • You sign in with Apple or Google. We store no password. The server verifies your identity token and issues short-lived access tokens (15 minutes) and rotating refresh tokens. Refresh tokens are stored only as hashes in our database.
  • Every request is checked against your account, and you can only access your own data.
  • Without valid health consent, the server blocks the features that process health data.
  • Limits on the number and size of requests to prevent abuse.
  • Secrets such as keys and tokens are removed from logs, and error reports are filtered (section 3.7).
  • The database is a managed Postgres service in the EU that encrypts stored data and takes backups.

We have no security certifications to report. No service is completely secure. If an incident puts you at risk, we will inform you and IMY as required by the GDPR.

12. Cookies, tracking and advertising

The app uses no cookies. It contains no advertising and no ad networks and shares no data for advertising. The app does not use Apple's tracking feature (ATT) and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. The phone's advertising identifier is not read.

The website tavana.app uses no cookies and no analytics, and processes no data about you beyond the technical access logs kept by its host Vercel (section 7).

13. Age

Tavana is for adults. You must be at least 18 to use the app. You confirm this on the consent screen before you can continue (section 4). We do not check age in any other way. If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we delete the account.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. For a material change we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. If the change requires new consent, the app shows the consent screen again. The date and version are at the top. We send earlier versions on request.
