2. What the service is

Tavana is a training app that gives training guidance based on how you feel and train. Among other things it can:

  • work out a daily readiness status (green, amber or red) from sleep, resting heart rate, HRV and your check-in, among other things,
  • suggest today's session or rest,
  • let you log sessions, build your own sessions and templates and follow programs,
  • show your training history, your progress per exercise and insights about your training.

The service is free in its current version. It is under development, and features may be added, changed or removed (see section 12).

3. No medical advice

Tavana is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The guidance and the readiness status are general training information. They do not replace a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian or other healthcare professional.

Readiness is not a health assessment. It compares your signals with your own baseline to plan training, and says nothing about your state of health. During the first weeks the app does not yet have a reliable baseline for you.

Some content is generated automatically, for example descriptions of exercises you add yourself and some sessions in programs. Such content can be incomplete or wrong. Review it and use your own judgement.

Tavana is not a medical device and must not be used to detect, monitor or treat disease.

  • In an emergency, call 112 (or your local emergency number).
