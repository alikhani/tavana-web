Common questions

When is the app coming?

Tavana is coming soon to iPhone through the App Store. An Android version will follow later.

What does it cost?

Tavana is free to get started.

Do I have to connect Apple Health?

No. With Apple Health, the suggestion can take sleep, HRV and resting heart rate into account. Without it, it is based on what you tell the app.

Is readiness medical advice?

No. It is a tool for how you set up today’s training. If you have symptoms or questions about your health, contact a healthcare provider.
