How it works

One suggestion a day, with a reason

  1. Tell us a little about you

    Three short steps about your goal, how and where you like to train and how often. No program starts automatically – you choose if and when.

  2. Connect Apple Health if you like

    With your permission, Tavana reads sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and steps. You can also use the app without it – the suggestion is then based on what you tell it.

  3. Do a short check-in

    How energetic and how stressed do you feel, how did you sleep, are you feeling sick? A few taps, no long forms.

  4. Get today’s suggestion

    Strength, conditioning, recovery or rest – and why. The workouts you log are taken into account when Tavana suggests the next day.

Readiness

Three states, no scores

Tavana compares today’s signals with your own baseline and sums them up in one of three states. The state shapes how today’s training is adjusted – nothing more.

  • Green

    Your signals look as usual. Train as you planned.

  • Amber

    Something stands out, such as short sleep or high stress. Train, but hold back a little today.

  • Red

    Your signals clearly differ from your baseline, or you said in your check-in that you are sick. A light session, recovery or rest is a better fit.

Readiness is a tool for planning your training, not a health assessment or medical advice. During the first weeks, Tavana is still learning your baseline. And you always decide.
